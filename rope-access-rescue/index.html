Rope rescue planner

Build a rescue plan for a rope access job. Pick the rigging setups that fit the job, and each one gets a drawing, a step-by-step method, a backup plan and an equipment list. Save it all as one PDF.

Register or sign in

Draft plan. Setups and steps follow the IRATA ICOP (TC-102) and TACS (TC-101, issue 006) rescue requirements, written in plain words. Check each plan against the site risk assessment and the equipment makers' instructions. The Level 3 supervisor must approve it and brief the team before work starts.

    3. Site drawing (optional)

    Add a picture or PDF of a drawing (general arrangement, plot plan, tank drawing) or a photo of the structure. The AI marks tank entries, work areas, anchor points, access routes and hazards with numbered pins. Check every pin: drag it to the right spot, rename it or delete it. Only use drawings you're allowed to share, because the picture is sent to Claude to read.

    4. Team

    IRATA: at least two technicians and one Level 3 supervisor. Three is the normal minimum unless every rescue is a straight lower to a safe place (ICOP 2.11.6).

    6. Rescue setups

    Tap a setup to see it, then tick "Include" to put it in the plan. IRATA prefers rig-for-rescue systems where they're possible (TACS 6.5.1). The level shown is the lowest IRATA level trained for that rescue. Confined space rescues are not part of IRATA training, so they need separate confined space training.
