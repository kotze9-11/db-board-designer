Privacy notice

DB Board Designer, operated by DM Energy. Draft for testing – to be reviewed before public launch.

What we collect

  • Your email address and a password (stored securely by our login provider, Supabase).
  • The boards you draw and save, including names, notes and circuit details.
  • Photos you upload to have a board read. A photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude service to read the board, and is not kept by us after it has been read.
  • A simple log of when you used photo reading, to apply a daily limit.

Why we collect it

Only to provide the service: to let you sign in, save your boards, and turn photos into drawings and reports. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

Supabase stores accounts and boards. Anthropic processes uploaded photos to read them. Both act on our behalf and may store data outside South Africa.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or close your account. You can delete any saved board yourself under "My boards". To close your account, contact DM Energy through dmenergy.co.za.

Important

Drawings and reports are based on photos and the information supplied. They are subject to on-site inspection by a registered electrician and are not a Certificate of Compliance.
