    Sizes follow the SANS 10142-1 current ratings for Surfix cable in conduit. Long runs, hot roof spaces or cables bunched together can need a thicker cable, so a registered electrician must confirm them for the CoC.

    Earth leakage

    Surge protection

    Breaker ratings

    Trip curve: B for lights and plugs, C for general use and small motors, D for big motors. Breaking capacity is the biggest fault the breaker can safely switch off. It must be at least the fault level at the board, so ask the electrician or the municipality. 6 kA is common for homes.

    Type A also picks up the pulsing DC leakage that inverters and electronics can cause, so it is the better choice with an inverter.

    Inverter and DC

    Lets you switch the backup circuits straight to the grid, so the inverter can be switched off, worked on or removed.

    Joins neutral to earth only while the grid is off, so the backup earth leakage still works. Most single-phase hybrid inverters need the contactor.
